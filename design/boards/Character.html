Intercat
The box is the cat. A cardboard box whose lid flaps are its ears, the face drawn straight on the front — pill-shaped amber eyes, marker whiskers, the tape for a nose line — and two detached paws underneath. The only cat-black is the paws and the tail. The flaps stand like a cat’s ears — short, wide, tilted out — and one has a corner folded over: the quirk you remember it by.

Construction
Four parts, like Duo’s four: a body of one shape (rounded box), big pill eyes that act by stretching, a silhouette nothing else has (box + flaps), and detached paws. Head and body are 1–2 base shapes; the triangle repeats in ears, nose and whisker angles.
Exaggerate to the edge of caricature: ears too big, whiskers too long, eyes that go from a flat pill to a tall one. Fewest details that still read; the silhouette gets its own space.
Voice
Lines stream in letter by letter (about 35 characters a second in English, 18 in Japanese); “more ›” appears when the line has finished. Each 2–3 characters make one short blip, synthesized in Web Audio — a soft, cat-ish tone around 600–900 Hz, 60–90 ms. A question lifts the last blip, a stop drops it low and short, a commit makes it high and light. Quiet, and silent whenever the mic is listening, during push-to-talk, or when the window is in the back. Follows system mute; a Sound toggle in Settings is separate from Mute.
Rules
Lives at the left end of the composer: your words go in past it, and the words it placed show above it. When it speaks, the line appears on the Editor’s own line directly above the composer, over its head — where the eye passes just before typing. The row is always there, never clickable, and never moves. “listening” shows there while the mic is on.
Speaks on three occasions only: first run; when it did something on its own (place, split, cut, stop, pause, mask, change mode, reconnect, add an Image track, try it two ways, switch to the local Glimmer, take a word back) or saw something through (a worker finishing, a commit or export, your answer on a card); and when tapped. Lines it would have said while you were away are held and delivered as one line when you return. Never modal.
Length: the line is one templated sentence about what happened (72 characters at most, guaranteed in code; no ellipsis). Everything it wants to say goes into the detail: “more ›” at the end of the line opens it in full above the line, and the reason is also kept in the worker’s log. It stays 4 s plus 50 ms per character, 12 s at most.
A cat, not a mascot: it never waves, bounces, or looks at the camera unless tapped. The biggest motion is the paw.
Color budget: cardboard (three values) for the body — the only warm surface in the app; amber for the eyes with a white catchlight when open; the UI’s dark gray for paws and tail. Ink-brown for whiskers and nose. No outlines anywhere.
Every state must read at 16px from the ears and the eyes alone.
Mutable from Settings. Muted, it still moves; only the lines stop.
